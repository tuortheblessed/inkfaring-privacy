OpenGround Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026 · Kyle Dellis

This policy covers the OpenGround app for iPhone and Apple Watch, including its widgets and watch complications.

The short version

OpenGround does not collect any data. The developer has no servers and cannot see your journal, todos, notes, habits or calendar. Everything stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud.

Where your data lives

  • On your device. Your journal entries, todos, notes, life areas and habit marks are stored in the app on your iPhone.
  • In your own iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app syncs that data through your private CloudKit database (iCloud.com.openground.app) so it appears on your other devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account and is protected by Apple. The developer cannot read it. If iCloud is off, your data stays on the device and nothing is synced.

Calendar

If you allow it, OpenGround reads events from the calendars you choose so it can show them on your day page, in widgets and on your Apple Watch. It only reads them and never changes your calendars. Event details stay on your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch. They are never stored in iCloud by OpenGround and never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Photos

OpenGround asks for add-only access to Photos, and only when you save a wallpaper. It adds the picture to your library and cannot see or read your existing photos.

Widgets and Apple Watch

The app, its widgets and the Watch app share data on your devices through a private app group (group.openground.shared). The iPhone and your paired Apple Watch exchange data directly using Apple’s WatchConnectivity. None of this passes through the developer.

What OpenGround does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking by the developer.
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs.
  • No data is sold or shared.

Exports and backups you make

You can export your journal as a zip of Markdown files and share it with the share sheet, and you can save wallpapers to Photos. These files are yours. Once you save or send them somewhere, such as Files, email or another app, that place’s own privacy terms apply. Device backups (for example iCloud Backup) are handled by Apple under your Apple Account.

Deleting your data

  1. Delete OpenGround from your iPhone. This removes the data on the device, and the Watch app with it.
  2. To remove the synced copy, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage (the wording varies by iOS version), choose OpenGround and delete its data.

Exports and wallpapers you saved elsewhere are not removed. Delete them where you saved them.

Children’s privacy

OpenGround collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
